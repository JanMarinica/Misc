What the logs show

  1. The test itself is 71–95 s; the seat-to-seat tact is 110–137 s. EOL20 and EOL21 (IO1978) test a seat in 71–76 s, the PLC testers of EOL1 and EOL5 need 86–95 s. Between seats there is another 24–48 s of handling and waiting, which is longest on EOL21 (48 s) and EOL5L (40 s).
  2. The PLC stations spend 10–12 s more on setup before the component tests. Lumbar starts 26–27 s into the test on EOL1/EOL5 and 15–18 s on EOL20/EOL21. Check assembly position takes 3.1–3.5 s against 0.2 s, grounding and isolation 6.0–6.3 s against 2.2–2.6 s, and PSM power-up 4.2 s against 2.9 s. On top of that, the PLC tree waits for Mood light (“Mood Light WTF” sits in the first background block), which holds the line for 3.7–3.9 s. On the IO1978 stations the same wait is placed after PSM power-up and costs nothing.
  3. Movements are the largest block, 36–47 s, about half of the test. Two stations stand out because of the laser “Check & Fix” correction: EOL1L backrest forward takes 15.4 s (others 7–8 s) and EOL5R length backward takes 11.8 s (others 6–7 s). EOL21 backrest forward is also high at 10.8 s. Height down takes 12.4 s on PLC stations against 10.3 s on IO1978.
  4. Background tests are fully hidden. Airbags, belt buckle, STPS, SBR, mood light and heating start together after short-to-ground and are all finished before Lumbar ends. The “wait until finished” before Movements is 0 s in the median on every station.
  5. Grounding sometimes repeats. The grounding and isolation phase takes more than 10 s in 21 % of seats on EOL5R, 13 % on EOL21, 11 % on EOL1R, and 5–8 % elsewhere (p90 up to 16 s).
  6. SPA-3 seats are rare and slow. 1–3 per station; 101–133 s of test time, of which the CAN calibration routine alone takes 55–72 s.
  7. Quality: 19 NOK seats out of 1 509. EOL1R had none; EOL5L had the most (7, mostly STPS and airbags).

Sequence and interlocks

The main tree runs one step after another. Background tests are started by a “Start” manager and collected by a “WTF” (wait to finish) manager; semaphores lock steps that must not overlap. The flow below is the SPA-1 tree, which covers 99 % of the seats.

flowchart TD
  A["Operator loads seat, scans barcode
(variant decides which tests run)"] --> B["Init, parameter calc,
check assembly position"]
  B --> C["Adapter plugged, connect ground
(isolation protection waits on Semaphore 3)"]
  C --> D["Short to ground"]
  D --> E["Grounding released"]
  E --> F{{"Start background tests"}}
  F -.-> P1["Airbags SAB / FSAB"]
  F -.-> P2["Belt buckle (operator plugs buckle)"]
  F -.-> P3["STPS"]
  F -.-> P4["SBR load / unload"]
  F -.-> P5["Mood light"]
  E --> G["PSM power-up, CAN/LIN on"]
  G -.->|starts| P6["Heating"]
  G --> H["PSM reset, start diagnostics
(waits for Heating)"]
  H --> I["Ventilation (option)
SBR load sync via Semaphore 1/2"]
  I --> J["Lumbar"]
  J --> K["Massage (option)"]
  K --> W{{"WTF: wait for all background tests"}}
  P1 & P2 & P3 & P4 --> W
  P5 -->|PLC: waited before power-up
IO1978: after power-up| G
  W --> M["Movements: height, length,
extension, tilt, backrest + laser Check & Fix"]
  M --> X["Expedition position"]
  X --> Y["CAN final check, clear DTCs, PSM reset"]
  Y --> Z["Finish: IO reset, unplug adapter, SFCS result"]

Station-dependent branches in the config: “gP” steps run on EOL1/EOL5 (PLC), “g7” steps on EOL20/EOL21 (IO1978). Robot steps (adapter connect, buckle, buttons, disconnect) only run with RobotAut on; on 14 Oct every cycle ran in manual mode, so they were bypassed.

Method

A cycle runs from “Starting test” of MainGroupOfTests to onTestsFinished. Component tests are the test groups the tester reports to SFCS (Short to ground, Airbags, Belt buckle, STPS, SBR, Mood light, Heating, Ventilation, Lumbar, Massage, Movements, Expedition position), matched by test Id, with PLC and IO1978 variants merged. Variant is read from the broadcast barcode (V = SPA-1, G = SPA-3). Scripts: ProcessAnalysis/analysis/parse_logs.py, analyze.py, build_report.py.
